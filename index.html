1.5-hour session companion

Before → during → after

Before

60-second body check

Notice breath, jaw, shoulders, chest, belly and hands. Choose one intention: make a clear request, notice before retreating, or stay with uncertainty.

During

Four channels

Body: sensation?
Emotion: feeling?
Impulse: retreat, freeze, please, hurry or control?
Action: what did I actually do?

After

Three-minute debrief

Strongest body signal. Most charged moment. What I did. What I’m curious about. One small real-life practice this week.

The horse is not a Rorschach test. Horse behaviour can be therapeutically interesting because of your reaction without assuming we know exactly what the behaviour “means” psychologically.
Evidence, not hype

Promising, but not settled

2025 systematic review

31 eligible studies across PTSD, anxiety and mood disorders; positive signals, but few strong randomised trials and major heterogeneity.

Social-anxiety pilot RCT

A very small randomised study combining equine-assisted activities with CBT. Interesting, not definitive.

Horse welfare review

28 welfare papers with inconsistent stress findings; horse welfare requires active observation rather than assumption.

# Books

A reading list for equine-assisted psychotherapy and related horse-human work. These are **links and summaries**, not copies of copyrighted books.

## 1. The Clinical Practice of Equine-Assisted Therapy — Leif Hallberg

**Best all-round clinical reference.** Hallberg bridges research and practice, cites more than 300 peer-reviewed articles, and covers terminology, competencies, clinical considerations, horse welfare and ethics.

Publisher: https://www.routledge.com/The-Clinical-Practice-of-Equine-Assisted-Therapy-Including-Horses-in-Human/Hallberg/p/book/9781138674622

Why it matters to a participant: it gives a more disciplined view of the field than popular claims about horses “healing” people automatically.

## 2. How Change Happens in Equine-Assisted Interventions — Noreen W. Esposito & Angela K. Fournier

**Best for understanding possible mechanisms of change.** The book examines what happens during equine-assisted interventions and develops theory around experience, memory, meaning, nervous-system processes, metaphor, reflection and psychotherapy.

Publisher: https://www.routledge.com/How-Change-Happens-in-Equine-Assisted-Interventions-A-Theory-of-Horses-Humans-and-Psychotherapy/Esposito-Fournier/p/book/9780367538507

For participant-focused reading, this is particularly relevant to the question: *how might an experience with a horse become useful psychological change outside the session?*

## 3. An Introduction to Equine Assisted Psychotherapy — Meg Kirby

**Best Australian introduction.** Kirby describes the Equine Psychotherapy Institute model with foundations in psychotherapy theory, therapeutic technique, professional integrity and respect for the horse.

Author page: https://megkirby.com.au/an-introduction-to-equine-assisted-psychotherapy/

Publisher/book details: https://www.balboapress.com/en-au/bookstore/bookdetails/714221-An-Introduction-to-Equine-Assisted-Psychotherapy

## 4. Equine-Assisted Mental Health for Healing Trauma — Kay Sudekum Trotter & Jennifer N. Baggerly (eds.)

A trauma-focused clinical collection covering childhood and complex trauma, dissociation, attachment, Somatic Experiencing, EMDR, veterans and post-traumatic growth, with case material.

Publisher: https://www.routledge.com/Equine-Assisted-Mental-Health-for-Healing-Trauma/Trotter-Baggerly/p/book/9780429456107

Useful but more clinically specialised than the first three books.

## 5. Equine-Facilitated Psychotherapy and Learning — Veronica Lac

**The Human-Equine Relational Development (HERD) Approach.** Practical and relational, with case studies involving trauma, attachment, depression, families and couples. It also addresses ethical and safety considerations and the welfare of both people and horses.

Publisher: https://shop.elsevier.com/books/equine-facilitated-psychotherapy-and-learning/lac/978-0-12-812601-1

## 6. Equine Therapy Exposed — Meg Kirby

Case-based follow-up material from the Australian Equine Psychotherapy Institute, showing the EPI/AWARE approach in practice.

Author page: https://megkirby.com.au/equine-therapy-exposed/

## 7. Nourished: Horses, Animals & Nature in Counselling, Psychotherapy & Mental Health — edited by Meg Kirby

A broad collection on incorporating horses, other animals and nature into counselling, psychotherapy and personal development. It includes contributions from several established animal-assisted and equine-assisted practitioners.

Author/editor page: https://megkirby.com.au/nourished-horses-animals-nature-in-counselling-psychotherapy-mental-health/

## Suggested order for a participant

If the goal is to understand weekly therapy rather than become a practitioner:

1. **An Introduction to Equine Assisted Psychotherapy** — accessible Australian framework.
2. **How Change Happens in Equine-Assisted Interventions** — deeper understanding of mechanisms.
3. **The Clinical Practice of Equine-Assisted Therapy** — strongest broad reference and ethics/research grounding.
4. Read trauma-specific or practitioner texts only where they become relevant.

## Buying/borrowing principle

Before buying an expensive clinical book, check local/public/university library catalogues or legitimate ebook services. Do not use pirated PDF copies. This repository deliberately stores bibliographic information and official links rather than copyrighted book files.

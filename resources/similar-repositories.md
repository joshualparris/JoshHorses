# Similar Horse-Therapy Repositories and Website Patterns

**Reviewed:** 13 September 2026

A GitHub search for `equine assisted therapy` and `horse therapy` found relatively few active public repositories specifically focused on equine-assisted mental health. Most are organisation websites rather than participant-facing knowledge bases.

## HopesHorseTherapy/website

https://github.com/HopesHorseTherapy/website

A public website repository for Hope's Horse Therapy. Its README is minimal and the project is best treated as a comparison for how a horse-therapy organisation presents itself online rather than as a research resource.

## FreedomRanch1/freedomranchinc.github.io

https://github.com/FreedomRanch1/freedomranchinc.github.io

A small public GitHub Pages repository connected with a horse-therapy organisation. Useful as another service-site reference.

## Angakess/horse-therapy-center

https://github.com/Angakess/horse-therapy-center

A larger horse-therapy-centre website codebase. Useful for interface/content-architecture comparison rather than clinical evidence.

## What JoshHorses is doing differently

The public repositories found are mostly **brochure/service websites**. JoshHorses is intentionally being developed as a **participant companion and evidence-aware resource library**, combining:

- one-tap podcast listening;
- a pre/during/post session framework;
- anxiety and assertiveness practice;
- searchable research and professional resources;
- Australian/Dubbo context;
- horse-welfare guidance;
- systematic reviews and sceptical evidence as well as supportive findings;
- printable resources.

This makes the project closer to a personal learning/PWA-style knowledge hub than a provider marketing website.

## External resource sites worth learning from

These are not GitHub repositories but are stronger content references:

- EAGALA — https://www.eagala.org/
- PATH International — https://pathintl.org/
- Horses and Humans Research Foundation — https://www.horsesandhumans.org/
- IAHAIO equine welfare guidance — https://iahaio.org/
- Equine & Animal Assisted Psychotherapy Institute (Australia) — https://www.equinepsychotherapy.net.au/
- Equine Assisted Therapy Australia — https://equineassistedtherapyaustralia.com.au/
- Animal Therapies Ltd — https://animaltherapies.org.au/

These sites are useful for standards, training, evidence, welfare and Australian professional context, but practitioner/model claims should still be separated from independent research evidence.

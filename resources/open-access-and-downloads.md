# Open-Access Articles, Manuals and Downloadable Resources

This page prioritises material that can be read or downloaded legitimately. It does **not** mirror copyrighted books or paywalled articles into this repository.

## Evidence — free full text

### 2025 systematic review: PTSD, anxiety and mood disorders

**Are Equine Assisted Interactions (EAI) a Dark Horse Bet? A Systematic Review on Measuring the Psychological and Physiological Effects of EAI on PTSD, Anxiety and/or Mood Disorders**

Free full text (PubMed Central):
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12410245/

PubMed record:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40865011/

Why keep it: this is currently one of the most useful high-level checks on both the promising findings and the weaknesses of the field.

## Professional standards and manuals

### PATH International Standards Manual

https://pathintl.org/resources/standards-manual/

PATH provides downloadable standards/summary materials covering equine-assisted services, safety, precautions and contraindications, terminology and accreditation/certification expectations.

### PATH Equine-Facilitated Psychotherapy and Equine-Assisted Learning manuals

Resource hub:
https://pathintl.org/topics/general/

Look for:

- **Equine-Facilitated Psychotherapy (EFP) Manual — Download**
- **Equine-Assisted Learning (EAL) Manual — Download**

### PATH Equine Specialist in Mental Health & Learning resources

https://pathintl.org/certification/mental-health-and-learning/

Includes candidate handbooks, criteria and ethics material.

### EAGALA Code of Ethics — PDF

https://www.eagala.org/wp-content/uploads/Eagala-Code-of-Ethics_revised__approved_11.7.20.pdf

Useful whether or not a practitioner uses EAGALA because it gives concrete examples of equine-assisted ethical concerns: human and horse welfare, scope, confidentiality, safety, referral and professional integrity.

### EAGALA research resources

https://www.eagala.org/equine-therapy-research/

Includes a research bibliography and research guidance.

## Australian resources

### PACFA specialist-course accreditation listing

https://pacfa.org.au/portal/portal/Training-and-Careers/Accreditation/Specialist-Courses.aspx

Useful for checking current recognition of Australian specialist psychotherapy training.

### Equine and Animal Assisted Psychotherapy Institute — Code of Ethics

https://www.equinepsychotherapy.net.au/code-of-ethics/

Particularly useful for distinguishing psychotherapy from non-therapy Equine Assisted Learning.

### Bendigo Health Carer Support — current EAL example

https://www.bendigohealth.org.au/css/events/

A useful public-health example of an Australian service describing equine-assisted learning as unmounted, psychologically/physically safe interaction with horses, with no horse experience required.

## Copyright note

A file being downloadable somewhere online does not automatically mean it can legally be redistributed in a public GitHub repository. For third-party books, articles, newsletters and course manuals, prefer a stable link to the rights-holder or legitimate open-access repository.

If a resource is clearly Creative Commons, public domain, authored by the repository owner, or permission is obtained from the rights-holder, a local copy can be added later with licence/provenance recorded beside it.

# Podcasts and Audio

Spotify-first listening for understanding equine-assisted psychotherapy, regulation, boundaries and horse-human interaction.

## Tier 1 — Start here

### Recovery Lab #143 — Healing Without Words with Stephanie Hathorn

**Approx. 54 minutes.** This is the strongest single introduction found for a participant rather than a clinician. Stephanie Hathorn discusses her own anxiety/panic/depression journey and explains equine-assisted psychotherapy through honesty, boundaries, trust, emotional awareness and regulation.

Spotify show: https://open.spotify.com/show/30WFJaTIti7LF3Hkblt8q8

Search in the show for: **Episode #143: Healing Without Words with Stephanie Hathorn**.

### Equine Assisted World with Rupert Isaacson

A dedicated show about equine-assisted practice across psychotherapy, learning, disability, horsemanship and related fields.

Spotify: https://open.spotify.com/show/3yDAMxxOmbLpzIwwQZKY6a

Recommended episode: **Misery Is Optional — Redefining Softness, Structure & Anxiety with Mark Rashid & Crissi McDonald**. Particularly useful for thinking about pressure, anxiety, clear requests and remaining responsive rather than forceful.

## Tier 2 — Therapy and somatic depth

### The Journey On Podcast — Jane Faulkner

Jane Faulkner is an Australian practitioner whose work draws on equine-assisted psychotherapy, Gestalt and Somatic Experiencing. Useful for embodiment, regulation and present-moment awareness.

Episode reference: https://podcasts.apple.com/au/podcast/jane-faulkner/id1525271300?i=1000632020982

If using Spotify, search: **Journey On Jane Faulkner**.

### The Baffling Behavior Show — Episode 51: Equine-Assisted Trauma Informed Psychotherapy

Robyn Gobbel interviews mental-health therapist Michael Remole about trauma-informed equine-assisted psychotherapy.

Episode page, with Spotify link: https://robyngobbel.com/podcasts/equinetherapy/

Good for understanding nervous-system vulnerability, safety and why experiential interaction may offer information that ordinary conversation does not.

## Tier 3 — Wider field

### Therapy Horse Talk — Sharne Brown

Australian mental-health social worker/family therapist discussing counselling, equine-assisted therapy and practice. Useful for an Australian context.

Search Spotify for: **Therapy Horse Talk Sharne Brown**.

### Taking Back the Reins — Dina Shale

Trauma-informed equine-facilitated learning with an emphasis on embodied recovery and horse-human interaction.

Search Spotify for: **Taking Back the Reins Dina Shale**.

## Listening rule

Do not treat podcast claims as established science merely because they sound intuitively right. In particular, claims that horses literally “read”, “absorb”, “synchronise with”, “diagnose” or “mirror” a human emotional state can easily outrun the evidence.

Use podcasts to generate good questions and awareness; use the systematic reviews and professional standards in [evidence-and-standards.md](evidence-and-standards.md) to judge stronger factual claims.

## Suggested sequence

1. Recovery Lab #143.
2. Equine Assisted World — Misery Is Optional.
3. Journey On — Jane Faulkner.
4. Baffling Behavior Show — Episode 51.
5. Browse Equine Assisted World according to the themes that become relevant in actual sessions.

The purpose of listening is not to arrive at therapy already knowing what is supposed to happen. It is to gain enough language to notice your own experience without forcing it to match someone else’s story.
